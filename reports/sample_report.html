Validation Boundaries & Caveats

  • Unmeasured Specificity: No non-CAD control patients exist in the training dataset (41 of 42 patients harbor lesions), so specificity is completely unmeasured.
  • External Scanners Untested: Generalization across external hospitals, cath labs, and x-ray equipment is untested.
  • Preprocessing Sensitivity: Detections are sensitive to letterbox padding and image scaling.
